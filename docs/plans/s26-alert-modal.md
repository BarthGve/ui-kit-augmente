# Plan — Story s26-alert-modal

> **Design approved by the owner (2026-10-09).** The owner reviewed `docs/designs/s26-alert-modal.md` + `.html` and the artifact page (version 23) and found them conforming. Decisions applied to the tasks below (they override any earlier wording of this plan): (1) layout and radius as in design revisions 1-3: title, muted message, divider, right-aligned footer, modal radius token `--c--components--modal--border-radius` (8px); (2) `severity?` is **optional with no default**; without it there is no icon and the primary button is the neutral inverse style; with it the icon and the semantic button colour appear, and `severity="error"` focuses the **dismiss** button initially (otherwise the primary action, else dismiss); (3) icons are Material ligatures like `MessageModal` (aria-hidden); (4) default labels reuse existing keys (`disclaimer.ok`, `confirmation.cancel`), no new locale key; (5) no `useModals().alertModal()` helper (follow-up story if wanted); (6) the primary action does not close the modal by itself; (7) `size?: "default" | "sm"` (maps to the narrower Modal size) and an optional `media` slot in the header replacing the severity icon; (8) footer actions at natural width, right-aligned, stacked with the primary on top on narrow screens.

## Target story
**As a** user of a La Suite application **I want** an alert modal that interrupts me with an important message and asks me to acknowledge or decide **so that** I do not miss a critical warning, error or confirmation. Complexity 2 in stories.md, 2 after research (a pre-built variant of `Modal` plus a small additive `Modal` extension; `AlertDialog` does not exist in react-aria-components 1.16.0).
Each ticket is **test-first inside the ticket**: specs are written and seen failing before the code, and the ticket is committed only when everything is green (no `it.fails` ships; the behaviours being added do not exist yet).
Acceptance criteria (docs/stories.md):
- AC1 `AlertModal` is an `alertdialog` named by its title and described by its message.
- AC2 Four severities (`info`, `success`, `warning`, `error`) with icon and semantic colour from tokens.
- AC3 Focus moves in (primary, else dismiss), is trapped, returns to the opener.
- AC4 Dismiss action, optional primary action; Escape and dismiss call `onClose`; outside click opt-in.
- AC5 Default labels from the five locale files.
- AC6 `Modal`, `MessageModal`, `ConfirmationModal`, `DeleteConfirmationModal` behave as before (their specs pass unchanged).

## The point everything turns on
**Build `AlertModal` on the existing `Modal` and forward `role` and the two aria props, instead of adopting react-aria's dialog.** The evidence: `AlertDialog` does not exist in react-aria-components 1.16.0, a RAC `Dialog` would add a second overlay engine beside react-modal (different portal, focus scope, aria-hiding) and break the `ModalProvider` stack, whereas react-modal already supports `role` and `aria` and `Modal` simply does not forward them. If the owner rejects this (or wants focus on the least destructive action for `error`), t02 tasks 2-3 change; t01 stays.
